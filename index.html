OUR MISSION

Practical action for students and communities.

  • Raise student concerns through constructive representation.
  • Promote quality education and knowledge.
  • Support student welfare and awareness.
  • Encourage equal opportunities.
  • Promote safe and respectful environments.
  • Develop leadership and innovation.
  • Encourage unity and cooperation.
  • Promote positive social contribution.

OUR VISION

“To build an educated, united and progressive student community where every student has the opportunity to learn, participate, lead and contribute to society.”
